# WooCommerce SEO & Performance Optimization

Practical, plain-language documentation for people who run, build or optimize WooCommerce stores.

This repository is where I keep the checklists, audit steps and templates I actually use in my own store and client work. It is written from an SEO, content and site-operations point of view rather than a developer point of view. Everything here is documentation, not software.

## Why this repository exists

WooCommerce SEO advice is usually spread across blog posts, support threads and half-remembered fixes. When you work on several stores, the same problems keep coming back: duplicate category URLs, thin product descriptions, bloated page weight, unindexed products, missing structured data. Writing the process down once makes it repeatable, reviewable and easier to hand to a client.

## Who it is for

- Store owners who want to fix the obvious SEO and speed problems themselves
- Freelancers and marketers who audit WooCommerce or WordPress sites
- Print-on-demand sellers with large catalogues and thin product pages
- Anyone who wants a written process instead of guesswork

## How this repository is organised

- `checklists/` — short, ordered checklists you can work through on a real store
- `guides/` — longer explanations of why a fix matters and how to verify it
- `templates/` — reusable structures for product copy, category copy and audit reports
- `schema/` — notes and worked examples for product and breadcrumb structured data

## Suggested starting point

1. Run through `checklists/product-page-seo-checklist.md` on your three best-selling products.
2. Read `guides/woocommerce-performance-basics.md` and measure before changing anything.
3. Record what you changed and the date. Most SEO work is only useful if you can tell what caused a change later.

## Scope and honest limitations

- I work in SEO, WordPress, WooCommerce, print-on-demand and e-commerce content. I am not a software engineer and nothing here should be read as engineering advice.
- I only document things I have applied on real stores or verified in official documentation.
- Where an idea comes from official Google, WordPress or WooCommerce documentation, I link to the source instead of reproducing it. All wording in this repository is my own.
- Search engines and WooCommerce both change. Treat every item as something to verify on your own store, not as a guarantee.

## Status

Started July 2026 and maintained gradually. Files are added when I have something tested and worth writing down, not on a schedule. Expect the checklists to change as I learn more.
