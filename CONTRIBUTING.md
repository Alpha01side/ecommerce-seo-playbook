# Contributing

Corrections and additions are welcome. This is a documentation repository, so contributing means writing, not coding.

## The most useful contribution

Telling me something here is wrong, and what you saw instead on your own store. Open an issue with the file name, the line or section, what it says, and what actually happened. Real counter-examples are more valuable than agreement.

## What belongs here

- Practical guidance someone can act on the same day
- Checklists that have been used on a real store
- Worked examples with the reasoning shown
- Corrections where a platform or a search engine has changed behaviour

## What does not belong here

- Text copied from blogs, forums, or official Google, WordPress or WooCommerce documentation. Link to sources instead of reproducing them.
- Promotional content, affiliate links, or tool recommendations written to sell something.
- Guarantees about rankings. Nothing here promises results and nothing added should either.
- Content generated wholesale by a language model and submitted without being checked against real experience.

## Style

Plain English, short sentences, and the reasoning included. Say what the trade-off is. If something only applies in certain conditions, name the conditions. Prefer "this usually works because" over "best practice is".

Use British or American spelling consistently within a file; do not mix them.

## How to submit

For a small correction, open an issue. For a larger change, fork the repository, make the change on a branch, and open a pull request explaining what you changed and why. Include how you know, whether that is a store you ran, a test you did, or a documented change from the platform.
