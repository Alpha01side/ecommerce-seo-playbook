# Product Copy Brief Template

A short brief to fill in before writing or rewriting a product page. The point is to stop yourself writing filler. If you cannot answer the first three questions, the page is not ready to be written yet.

Copy this file, rename it after the product, and fill it in. Keep the finished briefs; they become your own reference for what worked.

## Rules I hold myself to

- No claim on the page that I cannot support.
- No supplier description pasted in unchanged.
- No keyword repeated for the sake of repeating it.
- Nothing copied from a competitor page. If I find myself paraphrasing closely, I stop and go back to the brief.
