# Product Page SEO Checklist (WooCommerce)

Work through this one product at a time. It is written for real stores, so most items take a few minutes rather than a developer ticket. Start with the products that already earn impressions in Search Console, because those respond fastest.

## 1. Before you change anything

- [ ] Record the current URL, title tag, meta description and H1.
- [ ] Note clicks, impressions and average position for the last 28 days in Search Console.
- [ ] Note the current price, stock status and whether the product has variations.
- [ ] Save a screenshot of the page. You will want a before/after reference later.

## 2. URL and indexing

- [ ] The URL is short, readable and contains the product name, not a product ID.
- [ ] The URL does not repeat the category path unless you have a deliberate reason for it.
- [ ] The product is indexable: no noindex tag, not blocked in robots.txt, not behind a login.
- [ ] The product appears in the WooCommerce product sitemap.
- [ ] If the product replaced an older one, the old URL redirects with a 301 rather than returning a 404.

## 3. Title tag and meta description

- [ ] The title tag leads with what the product is, then the qualifier a buyer would type: material, size, style, colour, fit.
- [ ] The title reads like a human wrote it. No repeated keyword, no all caps, no filler brackets.
- [ ] The brand name goes at the end unless the brand is the reason people search.
- [ ] The meta description says who the product suits and what is included. Treat it as ad copy, not a keyword field.
- [ ] Titles and descriptions are unique across variations and similar products.

## 4. On-page content

- [ ] One H1 that matches the product name a customer would recognise.
- [ ] The first 150 words answer the buying question: what it is, who it is for, what makes this version different.
- [ ] Specifications are listed as actual attributes, not buried in a paragraph.
- [ ] Sizing, care, materials, print method or compatibility details are present when they affect the buying decision.
- [ ] Shipping and returns expectations are stated or linked.
- [ ] No duplicated supplier description reused across dozens of products. Rewrite at least the opening and the specification framing for each one.
- [ ] Common pre-purchase questions are answered on the page, in your own words.

## 5. Images and media

- [ ] The main image shows the product clearly on a plain background; supporting images show scale or use.
- [ ] File names are descriptive before upload, not IMG_4821.
- [ ] Alt text describes the image for someone who cannot see it. It is not a keyword list.
- [ ] Images are compressed and served at a sensible display size, and modern formats are used where supported.
- [ ] Images below the fold are lazy loaded; the main product image is not.

## 6. Variations and duplicate content

- [ ] Variations that do not have separate search demand stay on one product page.
- [ ] Variations that genuinely have their own demand get their own indexable page with unique copy.
- [ ] Filter, sort and search URLs are not indexable.
- [ ] Each product has one canonical URL, and it points to itself.

## 7. Structured data

- [ ] Product structured data is present and matches what a visitor sees: name, image, description, price, currency, availability.
- [ ] Review or rating markup only appears when real reviews exist on the page.
- [ ] Breadcrumb markup reflects the actual breadcrumb trail.
- [ ] The page validates in a structured data testing tool with no errors and no invented values.

## 8. Internal linking

- [ ] The product is reachable from its category in two clicks or fewer from the home page.
- [ ] Related products are linked with descriptive anchor text, not only through a carousel.
- [ ] At least one relevant guide, buying page or blog post links to the product using natural anchor text.
- [ ] The breadcrumb is present and matches the category the product actually belongs to.

## 9. Speed and stability on the product template

- [ ] Measure the product template, not just the home page.
- [ ] The main product image is the largest element and loads early.
- [ ] Layout does not shift as gallery, badges or review widgets load.
- [ ] Scripts you no longer use are not loading on product pages.
- [ ] Test on a mid-range phone over mobile data, not only on desktop.

## 10. After publishing

- [ ] Request indexing for the URL, then leave it alone.
- [ ] Add the date and a one-line note of what you changed to your own change log.
- [ ] Re-check clicks, impressions and position after 21 to 28 days and compare against your before figures.
- [ ] If nothing moved, look for a demand problem or a competing page of your own before rewriting again.

## Notes

This checklist reflects how I work on stores. It is not a ranking guarantee, and no checklist replaces having something worth buying on the page. If an item here contradicts current official documentation, trust the documentation and please open an issue so I can correct it.
